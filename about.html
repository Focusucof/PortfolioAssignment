About Me

Who I Am

Hello! My name is Devin Boodoo, and I am a Networking & IT Security student based in Toronto, Canada. I have a strong passion for technology and enjoy working close to the system. I spend a lot of time building and experimenting with software, networks, and security. When I'm not in class, I'm usually building something, breaking it, or trying to understand why computers behave the way they do.

In my free time, I enjoy playing CTFs, playing video games, DJing, and building my homelab.

My Homelab

My homelab is a collection of servers and networking equipment that I use to experiment with different technologies and configurations. It's a place where I can test new ideas and learn from my mistakes without affecting any too serious.

Currently I'm running a variety of services including a web server, a NAS, a self-hosted GitLab instance, an email server, an AI agent vm, as well as a few other Linux and Windows VMs
